Questions

Good to know

Is it free to join?

Yes. Joining your state's JobGroup costs nothing.

Do I need to install anything?

No. If you have WhatsApp, you're ready. The website only points you to the right group.

Which states are covered?

All 36 states and the FCT. Search for yours above, or tap a region to browse.

How do I stay safe when hiring or applying?

Meet in public or bring someone along for first meetings, agree the price before work starts, and never pay upfront fees to secure a job. Report anything suspicious to the group admin.

Can I post more than one job?

Yes. Post as many real jobs as you need. Repeat or spam posts are removed by admins.
